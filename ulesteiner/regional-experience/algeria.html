Regional experience

Algeria

5 representative matters handled by our lawyers and associated partners in Algeria.

  1. Advising a Consortium of Lebanese and international companies in tendering for a Seawater Desalination BOOT project in Algeria. Task included leading the legal team that was involved in setting up the financing schemes, reviewing the contractual documents, giving advice on several aspects of Algerian law and drafting the JV agreement.
  2. Advised national utility in connection with preparation of agreements for natural gas-fueled project.
  3. Advised a foreign investor on a Contract of Association in the gas sector.
  4. Advised an Arab bank based in Frankfurt/Main on credit line agreements and credit facilities with a major Algerian bank and Algerian oil companies.
  5. Advised formwork and scaffolding producer in setting up a trading company with 70% foreign ownership and related tax, foreign exchange and custom issues.
