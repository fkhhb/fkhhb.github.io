Syria

3 representative matters handled by our lawyers and associated partners in Syria.

  1. Advised and represented international consortium on Public Tender and dispute with Public Establishment of Electricity for Generation and Transmission (PEEGT), Damascus, related to the Deir Ali Combined Cycle Power Plant and a contract value of EUR 386 mio.
  2. Termination of a distributorship agreement for major Japanese motorcycle producer
  3. Legal representation of Syrian agent against German principal on dispute over Bid Bond issued to the Syrian Ministry of Construction for the supply of cement before high court of Frankfurt am Main and the higher regional court.
