Regional experience

Jordan

6 representative matters handled by our lawyers and associated partners in Jordan.

  1. Advised the Government of the Kingdom of Jordan in a multi-phase assignment that will result in the procurement of 300 MW of new capacity. Review the legal and regulatory framework applicable to foreign investment in the power sector, preparation of recommended changes to laws and regulations to facilitate development and financing of new power projects, and drafting of the implementation agreement, power purchase agreement, and fuel supply agreement.
  2. Representing a contractor joint venture regarding the supply and laying of a wastewater conveyor in Jordan, As-Samra Wastewater Project.
  3. Advised a world leading cloud computing service provider on trade law, import and export regulations in the Kingdom in connection with setting up a data center.
  4. Advised major Japanese motorcycle producer on spot based sales purchase agreement.
  5. Advised German Supplier on Supply Agreement of water pipes and pumps for water scheme project.
  6. Advising German producer of industrial sales on importer’s agreement with Jordanian trader.
