Arbitration

How to Set Aside Arbitral Awards in Egypt

Arbitration is widely used in international business as an efficient and final method of dispute resolution. One of its key features is that arbitral awards are intended to be final and binding. At the same time, most legal systems recognise limited judicial review to set aside awards under narrow circumstances.
