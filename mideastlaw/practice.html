Our story

MIDEAST | Law was founded and is managed by veteran international lawyers for legal services and tax advice in the MENA region. We have gathered experience in cross-border transactions for almost three decades and built trusted cooperation networks involving European and MENA lawyers, to the benefit of a broad portfolio of clients from North America, Europe and the MENA region.

Our long-standing professional experience allows us to combine our in-depth knowledge across a wide range of legal areas and business industries with a unique understanding of the inter-legal, inter-cultural and linguistic challenges that shape international business investments and cross-border transactions in the MENA countries.

We advise and represent

International and multinational corporations · Medium-sized companies (“Mittelstand”) · Business, management and financial services firms · Law firms, consultants, tax advisers and auditors · Leading family-owned businesses · Professional organisations · Private investors and private equity firms · Diplomatic missions and NGOs

Global networks

Backed by three worldwide alliances

To complement our activities and representation in the MENA region, we draw on three worldwide networks of which we are members.

CBBL

Cross Border Business Lawyers, based in Baden-Baden, Germany — the only worldwide network of German-speaking law firms, with member firms in 60 countries across Europe, Asia, North and South America.

GPSA

German Professional Services Alliance, Cologne, Germany — an alliance of independent, internationally operating audit and consulting firms active in 71 countries.

Legalmondo

Based in Verona, Italy — a platform of international legal services present in 49 countries with 130 independent lawyers, specialised in international contracts, cross-border investment, IP protection and litigation.
